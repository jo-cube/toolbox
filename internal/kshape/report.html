Observed, not authoritative

Gaps and occupancy describe the supplied first-to-last offset span. They do not reveal broker watermarks, retention causes, compaction causes, or records outside the stream.

Logical bytes

Payload bytes sum non-tombstone formatter lengths. They exclude Kafka record overhead, compression, indexes, replicas, and broker disk usage.

Approximate keys

Distinct non-null keys and records-per-key use mergeable HyperLogLog summaries (theoretical relative error {{.HLLRelativeError}}). Null keys are separate.
